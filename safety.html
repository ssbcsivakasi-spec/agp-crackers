Safety Information

Please read this before buying, storing or using any fireworks product.

Core safety points

    Before you buy

    Confirm your state, city and neighbourhood rules on permitted dates, times and product categories — especially for aerial items like rockets, which are restricted or banned in many areas.

    During use

    Light items on level, open ground away from buildings and dry vegetation. Keep a bucket of water or sand nearby, and never relight a dud — wait, then soak it.

    Storage

    Keep items sealed in original packaging in a cool, dry place away from heat sources. Never store in a vehicle or where children can reach.

    After use

    Let spent items cool fully, soak in water, then dispose of according to local waste guidance.
