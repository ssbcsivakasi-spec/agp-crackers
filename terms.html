Cookies & Checkout Details

When you accept cookies on this site, we store the name, contact number and delivery address you type into the checkout form in a cookie on your own device, for up to 30 days. This is done purely as a convenience so the form is pre-filled the next time you check out from the same browser.

This information is never transmitted to, or processed or stored on, any backend server. AGP Crackers does not operate a database of customer details — the only place these details exist is the cookie in your browser, which you can clear at any time by declining cookies, clearing your browser data, or contacting us. The only copy of your order details we ever receive is the message you choose to send us on WhatsApp when confirming an order.

If you decline cookies, checkout still works as normal — you'll simply need to re-enter your details each time you place an order.
